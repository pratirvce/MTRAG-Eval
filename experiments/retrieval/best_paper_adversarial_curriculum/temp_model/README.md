# SentenceTransformer based on BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5

This is a [sentence-transformers](https://www.SBERT.net) model finetuned from [BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5](https://huggingface.co/BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5). It maps sentences & paragraphs to a 768-dimensional dense vector space and can be used for semantic textual similarity, semantic search, paraphrase mining, text classification, clustering, and more.

## Model Details

## Usage

### Direct Usage (Sentence Transformers)

First install the Sentence Transformers library:

```bash
pip install -U sentence-transformers
```

Then you can load this model and run inference.
```python
from sentence_transformers import SentenceTransformer

# Download from the 🤗 Hub
model = SentenceTransformer("sentence_transformers_model_id")
# Run inference
sentences = [
    '|user|: how does the mls all star game work\n|user|: What is it called outside of the USA?\n|user|: Can women play it?\n|user|: Which is the most popular? \n|user|: Where does it originate from?\n|user|: Is American football derived from it?\n|user|: Where American football is popular?',
    "Monty Python's Life of Brian\nFollowing the withdrawal of funding by EMI Films just days before production was scheduled to begin , long - time Monty Python fan and former member of the Beatles , George Harrison , arranged financing for Life of Brian through the formation of his company HandMade Films .",
    'Presidio Terrace\nMany notable San Franciscans have lived in Presidio Terrace over the years , including San Francisco Mayor Joseph Alioto , former Speaker of the House Nancy Pelosi , United States Senator Dianne Feinstein and her husband , financier Richard C. Blum , and novelist and newspaper columnist Merla Zellerbach .',
]
embeddings = model.encode(sentences)
print(embeddings.shape)
# [3, 768]

# Get the similarity scores for the embeddings
similarities = model.similarity(embeddings, embeddings)
print(similarities)
# tensor([[1.0000, 0.3519, 0.3620],
#         [0.3519, 1.0000, 0.4390],
#         [0.3620, 0.4390, 1.0000]])
```
